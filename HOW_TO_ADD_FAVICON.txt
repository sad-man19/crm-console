CREATING A FAVICON FOR YOUR APP
================================

1. Create a favicon image (any of these formats):
   - favicon.ico  (best for browser compatibility)
   - favicon.png  (32x32 or 64x64 pixels)
   - favicon.svg

2. Save it in the SAME folder as your app.py and dashboard.html
   Path: C:\Users\sadma\Desktop\Classics fraud checker\favicon.ico

3. OR use a free online favicon generator:
   - https://realfavicongenerator.net/
   - https://www.favicon-generator.org/

4. Upload the file to your hosting (Render) in the same directory as your app.py.

5. The HTML already references:
   - <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico">
   - <link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="32x32" href="/favicon.png">
